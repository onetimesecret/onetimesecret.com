[SSO What](https://ssowhat.dev) is a collection of interactive demonstrations for
understanding authentication and enterprise SSO flows.

The walkthroughs cover social login, enterprise identity providers, SAML logout, SCIM
provisioning, protocol bridging, and home-realm discovery. They are educational,
forward-looking examples of SSO patterns, not demonstrations of current Onetime Secret
capabilities or a production service for real credentials or sensitive data.
