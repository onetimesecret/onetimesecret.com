We've reorganized the [documentation site](https://docs.onetimesecret.com) around how
people use the site: share secrets, manage your account, set up a custom domain,
or run your own instance. Updated guides replace outdated pages and fill gaps in
the documentation, with sections for getting started, billing, and security too.

If you self-host Onetime Secret, focused guides now cover Docker, Linux,
setting up a reverse proxy and TLS, running as a service, and a few others. Expanded
configuration guides cover authentication, email, sessions and cookies, secrets and keys,
and security headers. The
[environment variable reference](https://docs.onetimesecret.com/en/reference/environment-variables/)
is generated from the application's own documented settings.

The optional **Highlight AI edits** switch lets you see text labelled as AI-written during the
writing and editing process. These labels do not indicate accuracy or whether that text has been
reviewed by a person. And text without highlights may also be AI-written. Read more about
[how and why we use AI in these docs](https://docs.onetimesecret.com/en/contribute/text-provenance/).
