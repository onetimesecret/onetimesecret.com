The [documentation site](https://docs.onetimesecret.com) is now organized by task: sending a
secret, managing your account, running an organization, setting up a custom domain, or
installing and configuring your own instance. Task pages that cite the application source they
describe replace the old placeholder and outdated pages. The full change is in
[PR #459](https://github.com/onetimesecret/docs.onetimesecret.com/pull/459).

For self-hosters, the single installation page is now six guides: Docker, Linux, images and
variants, reverse proxy and TLS, running as a service, and verifying the install. The
[environment variable reference](https://docs.onetimesecret.com/en/reference/environment-variables/)
is generated from the application's `.env.reference`, and a weekly job reports when the two
drift apart. Configuration guides for authentication, email, sessions and cookies, secrets and
keys, and security headers are also live.

Pages now include a **Highlight AI edits** switch that marks prose recorded as AI-written. The
record is self-declared, so text without highlights may also be AI-written. See
[how and why we use AI in these docs](https://docs.onetimesecret.com/en/contribute/text-provenance/).
