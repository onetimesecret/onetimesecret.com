## It's the Real Deal™

Effective today, eligible nonprofits, charities, and educational institutions get 50% off our [Identity and Team plans](/pricing). This program has been quietly listed on our pricing page for a while. Now it's Prime-Time Official™.

## How to apply

1. Create a free account on your favourite regional site.
2. Add your organization's main website as a custom domain. You can add more domains later.
3. Follow the domain verification steps.
4. Contact us through the feedback link in the main navigation menu and we'll proffer up a promo code.

*(That last step is a bit tedious, we admit. We're working on streamlining it so you can get your code right away.)*

## Fool me once, you can't get fooled again

Eligibility is deliberately open. We periodically review program accounts against our [acceptable use policy](/privacy), just as we do for all accounts. So no funny stuff!
