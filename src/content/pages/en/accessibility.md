We want everyone to be able to use Onetime Secret, including people who use assistive technology or change their device's motion, color or language settings. This statement covers the onetimesecret.com website.

## Conformance status

We aim to meet the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) at level AA. The site has not been formally audited, so some parts may not meet every guideline yet.

## What the site does

- **Reduced motion.** If your device is set to reduce motion, the site turns off its animations, and the homepage heading stays on a single example.
- **Pausing motion.** Otherwise, the homepage heading cycles through a few examples when it comes into view. The pause button above the heading stops it at any point.
- **Screen readers.** Screen readers skip the changing words in the homepage heading and read one fixed sentence instead.
- **Light and dark themes.** The site follows your system's light or dark setting, and the theme button in the footer switches between the two.
- **Languages.** The site is available in English, German, Spanish and French. Each page declares its language, so screen readers pronounce it correctly.

## Known limitations

- The privacy policy and terms of service are in English only.
- This statement does not cover the Onetime Secret app at its regional addresses, such as eu.onetimesecret.com, or our documentation and status pages.

## Feedback

If something on this site is hard to use, or you need its content in another format, email [support@onetimesecret.com](mailto:support@onetimesecret.com) or use the [feedback form](/feedback).

## Technical details

The site relies on HTML, CSS, WAI-ARIA and JavaScript.
